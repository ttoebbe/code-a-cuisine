Recipe generation (Google Gemini)

The ingredients and preferences you enter are transmitted through our server to the AI interface Google Gemini (Google Ireland Ltd. or Google LLC, USA) in order to create the recipes. Processing outside the EU is possible in the course of this. Please do not enter any personal data into the input fields.

Recipe storage (Google Firebase)

Generated recipes are stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Google Ireland Ltd.) and are permanently visible to all visitors in the public recipe library of this website. There is no delete function; the recipes contain no information about you, unless you write personal data into the input fields.
